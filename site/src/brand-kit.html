Rules

  • Green is never decoration: it means passed, recommended or focused.
  • One light from above. Sheets and cards cast shadows downward; nothing glows.
  • Only real product output appears as imagery. No stock photos, no invented screens.
  • Body text needs APCA Lc 75: black on #e3c9a1 is 76.8, on #ecd6b3 83.7. The brief's #dac199 (72.2) is kept only for the desk.
