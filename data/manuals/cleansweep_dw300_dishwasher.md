# CleanSweep DW-300 Dishwasher — User Manual

Model: DW-300 (13 place settings, freestanding). This manual covers installation, loading, programs, care and troubleshooting.

## Safety Instructions

- Only use detergent made for household dishwashers. Never use washing-up liquid; it creates foam that can leak from the machine.
- Keep dishwasher detergent out of the reach of children. It is corrosive.
- Do not sit or stand on the open door.
- Load knives and other sharp utensils with the points facing down in the cutlery basket.
- The water inside the dishwasher is not drinkable.

## Installation

- Connect the dishwasher to a cold or hot water supply with a maximum temperature of 60°C.
- Water pressure must be between 0.04 MPa and 1 MPa.
- The drain hose must not be higher than 1 metre above the floor.
- The dishwasher needs a socket with 10 A protection.

## Loading the Dishwasher

- Upper basket: glasses, cups, small bowls and plastic items marked as dishwasher safe.
- Lower basket: plates, pots, pans and serving dishes. Place large items at the sides so that they do not block the spray arms.
- Remove large food leftovers before loading. You do not need to rinse dishes under running water.
- Do not wash wooden boards, cast-iron pans, crystal glass or items with gold decoration in the dishwasher.

## Programs

| Program | Temperature | Duration | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intensive | 70°C | 2 h 40 min | Heavily soiled pots and pans |
| Auto | 45-65°C | 2 h 10 min | Mixed loads; sensors adjust the cycle |
| Eco | 45°C | 3 h 55 min | Normally soiled dishes, lowest energy use |
| Glass | 40°C | 1 h 45 min | Lightly soiled glasses |
| Quick 30 | 40°C | 30 min | Lightly soiled dishes, no dried-on food |
| Pre-Rinse | Cold | 15 min | Rinsing dishes you plan to wash later |

The Eco program uses the least energy and water. It runs longer because it uses lower temperatures and longer soaking.

To start a delayed program, press Delay repeatedly to choose a delay of 1 to 24 hours, then press Start.

## Salt and Rinse Aid

The water softener needs dishwasher salt. Fill the salt container at the bottom of the dishwasher only with coarse dishwasher salt; never use table salt. Before the first wash, pour 1 litre of water into the salt container, then add about 1.5 kg of salt.

Fill the rinse aid dispenser when the rinse aid indicator lights up. The rinse aid setting can be adjusted from 1 to 6. Increase the setting if glasses have water spots; decrease it if glasses have streaks.

## Care and Maintenance

- Clean the filters once a week. Turn the cylindrical filter anticlockwise, lift it out, and rinse the filter assembly under running water with a soft brush.
- Check that the spray-arm holes are not blocked. Remove the spray arms and rinse them if necessary.
- Run an empty Intensive program with dishwasher cleaner once a month.

## Error Codes and Troubleshooting

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Water inlet problem | Check that the water tap is open and that the inlet hose is not kinked. |
| E3 | Water is not heating | Contact service. |
| E4 | Water leak detected (overflow) | Turn off the water tap, unplug the dishwasher and contact service. |
| E9 | Button stuck | Make sure nothing presses against the control panel. |

If dishes are not clean, check that the filters are clean, the spray arms can turn freely and that you are using the right program.

If there is white residue on dishes, check that the salt container is filled and the water hardness setting is correct.
