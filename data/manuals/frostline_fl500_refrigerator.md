# FrostLine FL-500 Frost-Free Refrigerator — User Manual

Model: FL-500 (500 litre, double door, frost-free). This manual covers installation, temperature control, care and troubleshooting.

## Safety Instructions

- Wait at least 4 hours after delivery before switching the refrigerator on. This lets the refrigerant oil settle after transport.
- Do not store flammable aerosols or explosive substances in the refrigerator.
- Do not use electrical appliances inside the food compartments.
- Do not damage the refrigerant circuit at the back of the appliance. The refrigerant R600a is flammable.
- Unplug the refrigerator before replacing the interior LED or cleaning the condenser.

## Installation

- Leave at least 10 cm of space above the refrigerator and 5 cm at the back and sides for ventilation.
- Do not install the refrigerator next to an oven, a radiator or in direct sunlight.
- The appliance is designed for room temperatures between 10°C and 43°C (climate class SN-T).
- Level the refrigerator using the two adjustable front feet so that the doors close by themselves.

## Temperature Settings

The recommended setting is 4°C for the fridge compartment and -18°C for the freezer compartment.

The fridge compartment can be set from 2°C to 8°C. The freezer compartment can be set from -16°C to -24°C. Press the Fridge or Freezer button repeatedly to change the temperature in steps of 1°C. The display shows the set temperature, not the measured temperature.

After switching on for the first time, wait 24 hours before loading the freezer with food so that it can reach its set temperature.

## Special Functions

- Super Cool: lowers the fridge to 2°C for 6 hours. Use it after loading a large amount of fresh food. It switches off automatically.
- Super Freeze: runs the freezer at its coldest setting for 50 hours. Switch it on 24 hours before freezing more than 5 kg of fresh food.
- Holiday Mode: keeps the fridge at 15°C to save energy while you are away. The freezer keeps running at its set temperature. Remove all food from the fridge compartment before using Holiday Mode.
- Door alarm: beeps if a door stays open for more than 2 minutes.

## Care and Maintenance

- Clean the inside with lukewarm water and a little mild detergent. Do not use abrasive cleaners or solvents.
- Clean the condenser coils at the back with a soft brush or vacuum cleaner every 6 months.
- The water filter for the ice maker must be replaced every 6 months. Turn the old filter anticlockwise to remove it.
- The appliance defrosts automatically. You do not need to defrost it manually.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Possible cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Refrigerator does not cool | Power off, or the temperature is set too warm | Check the plug and fuse. Set the fridge to 4°C. |
| Water on the floor | Defrost drain is blocked | Clean the drain hole at the back of the fridge compartment with the cleaning tool provided. |
| Ice builds up in the freezer | Door not closed properly | Check the door seal for damage and make sure nothing blocks the door. |
| Loud clicking noise | The compressor is starting or stopping | This is normal. |
| Display shows F1 | Fridge temperature sensor fault | Contact service. |
| Display shows F2 | Freezer temperature sensor fault | Contact service. |

A gurgling or bubbling sound is normal. It is made by the refrigerant flowing through the circuit.

## Energy-Saving Tips

- Let hot food cool down before putting it in the refrigerator.
- Defrost frozen food in the fridge compartment. It helps keep the fridge cold.
- Do not open the doors more often or for longer than necessary.
