# BreezeCool AC-12 Split Air Conditioner — User Manual

Model: AC-12 (1 ton / 12,000 BTU inverter split air conditioner). This manual covers operation, remote control, care and troubleshooting.

## Safety Instructions

- Installation must be carried out by an authorised technician. Do not install, move or repair the unit yourself.
- Do not insert fingers or objects into the air outlet. The fan turns at high speed.
- Switch off the unit and unplug it during thunderstorms.
- Use a dedicated circuit with a 16 A circuit breaker.
- The refrigerant R32 is mildly flammable. Keep open flames away from the indoor and outdoor units.

## Operating Modes

- Cool: cools the room to the set temperature. The temperature can be set from 16°C to 30°C.
- Dry: removes moisture from the air with gentle cooling. Use it on humid days.
- Fan: circulates air without cooling.
- Heat: warms the room. Heating works at outdoor temperatures down to -7°C.
- Auto: selects Cool, Dry or Heat automatically based on the room temperature.

For energy saving, set the temperature to 24°C in Cool mode. Each degree lower increases energy consumption by about 6%.

## Remote Control

- Turbo: runs the fan at maximum speed for 20 minutes to reach the set temperature quickly.
- Sleep: raises the set temperature by 1°C after 1 hour and by another 1°C after 2 hours, for comfortable sleep.
- Timer: press Timer to switch the unit on or off automatically after 1 to 24 hours.
- iFeel: the remote measures the temperature where you are sitting. Keep the remote within 8 metres of the indoor unit and point it at the unit.
- The remote uses two AAA batteries. Replace both batteries at the same time.

## Care and Maintenance

- Clean the air filters every 2 weeks. Open the front panel, pull the filters down and out, wash them in lukewarm water below 40°C and let them dry in the shade before refitting them.
- Never run the air conditioner without the air filters.
- Have the indoor and outdoor units serviced by a technician once a year, ideally before summer.
- Keep the outdoor unit free of leaves, dust and other obstructions.

## Error Codes and Troubleshooting

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Indoor room temperature sensor fault | Contact service. |
| E5 | Outdoor unit protection (overload or high pressure) | Switch the unit off, check that nothing blocks the outdoor unit, wait 10 minutes and restart. |
| E6 | Communication fault between indoor and outdoor units | Contact service. |
| H1 | Defrosting in Heat mode | This is normal. Heating resumes automatically after a few minutes. |

If the unit is not cooling well, clean the air filters, close the doors and windows, and check that the set temperature is lower than the room temperature.

If water drips from the indoor unit, the drain pipe may be blocked or the unit may not be level. Contact service.

A white mist coming from the indoor unit in Cool mode is normal in humid conditions.
