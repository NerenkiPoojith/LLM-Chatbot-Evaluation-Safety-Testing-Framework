# QuickHeat MW-20 Microwave Oven — User Manual

Model: MW-20 (20 litre, 800 W solo microwave). This manual covers safety, cooking, care and troubleshooting.

## Safety Instructions

- Never operate the microwave when it is empty. Running it empty can damage the magnetron.
- Do not heat eggs in their shells or whole hard-boiled eggs. They can explode, even after heating has finished.
- Do not use metal containers, aluminium foil or dishes with gold or silver trim. They can cause sparks.
- Liquids can boil over suddenly after heating (delayed boiling). Stir liquids before and after heating and let them stand for 30 seconds.
- Do not try to repair the microwave yourself or remove the outer casing. The appliance contains high voltage, even when unplugged.

## Installation

- Place the microwave on a flat, stable surface at least 85 cm above the floor.
- Leave at least 20 cm of free space above the microwave and 10 cm at the back and sides.
- Do not block the ventilation openings.

## Control Panel

- Power levels: 100% (High), 80% (Medium High), 50% (Medium), 30% (Medium Low / Defrost) and 10% (Low).
- Press the Power button repeatedly to choose a power level, turn the dial to set the time, then press Start.
- Quick Start: press Start once to cook at 100% power for 30 seconds. Each additional press adds 30 seconds.
- Child lock: press and hold Stop for 3 seconds until the lock symbol appears. Press and hold Stop for 3 seconds again to unlock.
- The maximum cooking time is 95 minutes.

## Defrosting

Use the Defrost function by weight. Press Defrost, turn the dial to set the weight of the food from 100 g to 2 kg, then press Start. The microwave beeps halfway through so that you can turn the food over.

## Suitable Cookware

| Material | Microwave safe? |
|---|---|
| Heat-resistant glass | Yes |
| Microwave-safe plastic | Yes |
| Ceramic without metal trim | Yes |
| Paper plates (short heating only) | Yes |
| Metal pans and aluminium foil | No |
| Melamine | No |

## Care and Maintenance

- Clean the inside after each use with a damp cloth. To remove stubborn dirt and smells, heat a bowl of water with lemon juice at 100% for 5 minutes, then wipe the inside.
- Remove the glass turntable and wash it in warm soapy water or in the dishwasher.
- Keep the door seal and door surfaces clean so that the door closes properly.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Microwave does not start | Check that the door is fully closed and the child lock is not active. |
| Sparks inside the oven | Remove any metal objects. Check that the mica cover on the inside wall is clean and undamaged. |
| Turntable does not turn | Check that the roller ring is placed correctly under the turntable. |
| Food heats unevenly | Stir the food during cooking and use a lower power level for longer. |
