# AquaWave WM-7000 Front-Load Washing Machine — User Manual

Model: WM-7000 (7 kg capacity). This manual covers safety, installation, everyday use, care and troubleshooting.

## Safety Instructions

- Always unplug the washing machine before cleaning or servicing it.
- Do not wash items that have been soaked in petrol, solvents or other flammable substances.
- Keep children and pets away from the door while the machine is running. The door glass can reach 70°C during hot washes.
- Never use bleach in the main detergent drawer. Diluted bleach may only be added to the compartment marked with a triangle symbol.
- Connect the machine to an earthed socket rated at 10 A or more. Do not use extension leads.

## Installation

1. Remove the four transit bolts from the back panel before first use. Keep them for future transport. Running the machine with the transit bolts fitted can cause severe damage that is not covered by the warranty.
2. Place the machine on a firm, level floor. Adjust the front feet until the machine is level and does not rock.
3. Connect the inlet hose to a cold-water tap with a 3/4 inch thread. Water pressure must be between 0.05 MPa and 1 MPa.
4. Hook the drain hose into a standpipe or sink. The end of the drain hose must be between 60 cm and 100 cm above the floor.
5. Leave a gap of at least 2 cm between the machine and the walls or cabinets.

| Drum Clean | Empty | 90°C | 1 h 20 min |

The Quick 15 program is designed for lightly soiled clothes only. Use no more than 20 ml of detergent for Quick 15.

To activate the child lock, press and hold the Temp and Spin buttons together for 3 seconds. A padlock icon appears on the display. Repeat the same action to switch the child lock off.

## Detergent Drawer

The drawer has three compartments: I for pre-wash detergent, II for main-wash detergent, and the flower symbol for fabric softener. Do not fill fabric softener above the MAX line. Use low-foam detergent designed for front-loading machines.

## Care and Maintenance

- Run the Drum Clean program once a month with the drum empty to remove residue and odours.
- Clean the drain pump filter every 3 months. The filter is behind the small flap at the bottom right of the front panel. Place a shallow tray underneath, unscrew the filter anticlockwise, remove lint and coins, then screw it back in tightly.
- Leave the door and detergent drawer slightly open after each wash so that the drum can dry and mould does not develop.
- Wipe the door seal with a dry cloth once a week.

## Error Codes and Troubleshooting

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| E10 | Water is not filling | Check that the tap is open and the inlet hose is not kinked. Clean the inlet hose filter. |
| E21 | Water is not draining | Clean the drain pump filter and check that the drain hose is not blocked or kinked. |
| E30 | Door is not locked | Close the door firmly until it clicks. If the error stays, contact service. |
| E40 | Unbalanced load | Redistribute the laundry, or add a few more items, and restart the program. |
| E90 | Electronics fault | Unplug the machine for 10 minutes, then try again. If the error returns, contact service. |

If the machine vibrates strongly during spin, check that the transit bolts have been removed and that the machine is level.

If clothes come out with white residue, you are using too much detergent or the wrong type. Reduce the dose and use detergent for front-loading machines.

## Warranty

The AquaWave WM-7000 has a 2-year parts and labour warranty. The inverter motor has a 10-year warranty. The warranty does not cover damage caused by transit bolts that were not removed, incorrect installation, or the use of non-original spare parts.
